# Benjamin Nathan Bell — AI Actor Prior Art Documentation

## Summary

This repository documents the verified creation timeline of **Benjamin Nathan Bell**, a fully AI-generated actor created by independent filmmaker, director, and former Stability AI consultant **Heather Ferreira** (Silent House Good Habits Filmworks, later TTN/El Camino Unreal Studios, LLC) on **March 21, 2023**.

This predates by nearly two years the February 9, 2025 creation date of Tilly Norwood, a separately created AI character whose creators publicly claimed the title of "first AI actress." This repository exists to establish and preserve the factual public record.

## About Benjamin Nathan Bell

Benjamin Nathan Bell is a fully AI-generated actor and the lead starring actor in:

- *The Fragrance of Petrichor* (2024) — Two-time award-winning feature film
- *Atlantis: The Motion Picture* (2026) — Premiering July 2026
- Multiple TTN television productions including sitcom, variety, and children's programming

Benjamin Nathan Bell was created, developed, and publicly debuted by Heather Ferreira under Silent House Good Habits Filmworks, later renamed TTN/El Camino Unreal Studios, LLC, originally based in La Jolla, California, then San Francisco, California.
